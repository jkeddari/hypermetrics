## Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about using Hypermetrics.

## Getting Started

### What is Hypermetrics?

Hypermetrics is a real-time analytics platform for the **Hyperliquid protocol**. We provide comprehensive tracking of leaderboards, wallet addresses, large transactions, and custom alerts to help traders make informed decisions.

### Is there a free plan?

Yes! Our Free plan includes:
- Leaderboard access
- Basic address lookups
- Top 10 large transactions
- Community updates
- No credit card required

### Do I need a Hyperliquid account to use Hypermetrics?

No! Hypermetrics is a read-only analytics tool. You can track any public address on Hyperliquid without needing your own wallet or account on the protocol.

### How accurate is the data?

100% accurate. All data comes directly from the Hyperliquid blockchain. We don't estimate or interpolate - everything you see is real on-chain data with <100ms latency.

## Using the Platform

### How do I add an address to my watchlist?

1. Find an address (from leaderboard or search)
2. Click the address to view details
3. Click **"Add to Watchlist"** or the ⭐ icon
4. Optionally add a custom label

That's it! You'll now see updates for this address in your dashboard.

### How many addresses can I track?

Depends on your plan:
- **Free**: Basic lookups only (no persistent watchlist)
- **Basic**: Up to 50 addresses
- **Pro**: Unlimited addresses

### Can I track my own wallet?

Absolutely! Many users track their own addresses to:
- Monitor their performance
- Get alerts on their positions
- Compare against top traders
- Track historical metrics

### What's the difference between tracking and alerts?

**Tracking** = Adding an address to your watchlist for easy monitoring

**Alerts** = Getting notified when specific conditions are met (requires Basic or Pro plan)

You can track addresses without setting up alerts, but alerts require tracking first.

## Alerts & Notifications

### How do alerts work?

Alerts notify you when tracked addresses meet certain conditions:
- Transaction size thresholds
- Position changes
- PnL milestones
- Custom conditions

Set them up from any tracked address in your watchlist.

### What notification methods are available?

- **Email**: All plans (if you have alerts)
- **Push notifications**: Basic and Pro plans
- **Webhooks**: Pro plan only

### How do I avoid alert fatigue?

Start conservative:
1. Track fewer addresses (3-5 initially)
2. Set higher thresholds ($100k+ for transactions)
3. Use email for most alerts
4. Reserve push notifications for critical events only
5. Review and adjust weekly

### Can I temporarily disable alerts?

Yes! Go to Settings → Notifications and toggle alerts on/off. Your alert configurations are saved - you can re-enable them anytime.

Yes! Historical data access varies by plan:

### Can I export my data?

**Basic and Pro only**: Export tracked addresses and transaction data in CSV or JSON formats.

Free plan users can view data in-app but cannot export.

## Privacy & Security

### Is my watchlist private?

Yes! Your tracked addresses and alerts are:
- Completely private
- Only visible to you
- Never shared with others
- Encrypted in storage

We never sell or share your watchlist data.

### What data do you collect?

We collect:
- Email (for login)
- Watchlist data (addresses you track)
- Alert configurations
- Usage analytics

We DON'T collect:
- Trading credentials
- Private keys
- Personal financial data
- Unnecessary tracking data

See our [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy) for details.

### Can I delete my account?

Yes. Go to Settings → Delete Account.

**Warning:** This permanently deletes:
- Your account
- All watchlists
- All alert configurations
- All historical data

Cannot be undone. Export your data first if you want to keep it.

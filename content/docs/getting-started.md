## Quick Start Guide

Getting started with Hypermetrics takes less than 2 minutes. Let's walk through setting up your dashboard.

## Step 1: Create an Account

1. Click **"Start Tracking"** on the homepage
2. Sign up with your email or use Google/GitHub
3. Verify your email address
4. You're in!

## Step 2: Explore the Leaderboard

Once you're logged in, you'll see the Hyperliquid leaderboard - a real-time ranking of top performers.

### Understanding the Leaderboard

The leaderboard displays:
- **Trader addresses**: Wallet addresses of top performers
- **PnL**: Profit and Loss over different timeframes (24h, 7d, 30d, all-time)
- **Volume**: Total trading volume
- **ROI**: Return on investment percentage
- **Win rate**: Percentage of profitable trades
- **Rank**: Current position in the rankings

### Filtering Options

Sort and filter by:
- **Timeframe**: 24h, 7d, 30d, all-time
- **Metric**: PnL, Volume, ROI, Win rate
- **Minimum volume**: Filter out low-volume traders

## Step 3: Track Your First Address

Found an interesting whale or want to monitor a specific address? Let's add it to your watchlist.

### How to Add an Address

1. Click on any address from the leaderboard, OR
2. Use the search bar to enter a specific address
3. Click **"Add to Watchlist"** or the ⭐ icon
4. (Optional) Add a custom label for easy identification

That's it! You'll now receive updates on this address's activity.

## Step 4: Set Up Your First Alert

Alerts are the key to staying ahead. Get notified when important events happen.

### Creating an Alert

1. Go to your watchlist
2. Click on a tracked address
3. Click **"Create Alert"**
4. Choose your alert type:
   - **Transaction size**: Get notified for transactions above a certain value
   - **Position change**: Alert when opening/closing positions
   - **PnL threshold**: Track significant gains or losses
   - **Custom conditions**: Advanced users can set complex triggers
5. Select notification method (email, push, webhook)
6. Click **"Create Alert"**

### Alert Best Practices

**Start Simple**: Begin with basic transaction alerts
- Set a reasonable threshold (e.g., >$100k)
- Don't over-alert yourself

**Monitor Smart Money**: Track addresses with proven track records
- Check their historical performance first
- Focus on consistent performers, not one-hit wonders

**Use Labels**: Organize your watchlist
- "Top Traders", "Whales", "Competitors", etc.
- Makes reviewing alerts much easier

## Common Beginner Questions

### How often is data updated?

Real-time! Our infrastructure delivers updates with <100ms latency. Basic and Pro plans get priority refresh rates.

### What counts as a "large transaction"?

This varies by market conditions, but generally:
- **Small**: $10k - $50k
- **Medium**: $50k - $250k
- **Large**: $250k - $1M
- **Pro**: $1M+

You can customize these thresholds in settings.

### Can I track my own address?

Absolutely! Many users track their own wallets to:
- Monitor portfolio performance
- Get alerts on their own positions
- Compare against top traders
- Track historical metrics

### How do I avoid alert fatigue?

Start conservative:
1. Track fewer addresses initially (3-5)
2. Set higher thresholds
3. Use email for less urgent alerts
4. Reserve push notifications for whales only
5. Review and adjust weekly

### What if I see suspicious activity?

Hypermetrics is a read-only analytics tool. We display on-chain data but don't interact with the blockchain. If you notice concerning patterns:
- Use it to inform your own decisions
- Share insights with the community (if you're comfortable)
- Remember: past performance ≠ future results
